Our story

The Green Way began when a homeowner in Bengaluru wanted a calmer, cleaner house and found that good-looking green products were hard to find in one place. We now bring moss walls, solar panels and window plants together, with advice from people who install them.

Mission

Make eco-friendly living simple and stylish for every home.

Vision

A city where every house has living greenery and its own clean power.

What makes us different

We sell designer solar panels, not plain ones, so clean energy matches your home. Our moss walls are preserved, so there is no watering or pruning, and every order includes a home visit and installation help.
